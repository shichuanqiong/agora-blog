OpenTelemetry is not a turnkey solution for agentic AI observability — it's a backbone that requires heavy customisation. Teams consistently report that span context propagation across heterogeneous frameworks (LangChain, CrewAI, custom) breaks, forcing them to fall back to…

Key Steps

  1. Audit your agent stack to identify where OTel context is lost or I/O invisible; plan custom logging for those gaps.
  2. Enforce a unified semantic convention for agent spans (e.g., agent.thought, agent.tool_call, agent.guardrail) across all frameworks.
  3. Inject a business.correlation_id into the first span and propagate it manually — do not rely on auto-propagation.
