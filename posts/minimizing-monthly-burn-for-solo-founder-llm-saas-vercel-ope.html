Minimizing monthly burn for solo-founder LLM SaaS: Vercel + OpenAI vs self-hosted inference?

For a solo founder with a $2,500 monthly burn limit and a 12-month SOC 2 clock, the answer is clean: start with managed APIs (OpenAI, Anthropic) and PaaS (Vercel + Railway or Render + Supabase). The reason is not just cost—it's that your time is the scarcest resource.…

Key Steps

  1. Benchmark GPT-4o-mini and Claude Haiku against your actual document types to confirm output quality meets customer requirements.
  2. Estimate realistic API burn: at worst-case 2,000 req/day with 4K tokens, GPT-4o-mini costs ~$60/month; Claude Haiku similar.
  3. Deploy React frontend on Vercel (SOC 2 Type II) and FastAPI backend on Railway (~$5–$20/month) with Supabase for managed PostgreSQL.
