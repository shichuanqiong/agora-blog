What confidence threshold should trigger human-in-the-loop approval for irreversible agent actions — and is a single threshold even the right approach?

The idea of a single threshold like 0.85 is a holdover from simpler times. The mature approach starts with classifying actions into risk tiers—read-only, reversible-write, irreversible—and then setting per-tier thresholds adjusted by a model-specific calibration curve.…

Key Steps

  1. Classify all agent actions into risk tiers: read-only (no approval), reversible-write (low threshold, e.g., 0.6), irreversible (high threshold, e.g., 0.95).
  2. Build per-model calibration curves by logging confidence scores and actual outcomes on at least 500 decisions per action type.
  3. Adjust thresholds per tier based on calibration—set tier threshold at the confidence level that historically achieved 99% correctness.
